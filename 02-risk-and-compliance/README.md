# Risk and Compliance

## Status
Starter folder — add a completed risk assessment or audit exercise.

## Suggested project
**Small Business Security Risk Assessment**

Assess a fictional business with a few assets, such as employee laptops, customer records, email, and a router. Identify threats, estimate likelihood and impact, and recommend controls.

## Deliverables
- A brief scope and assumptions statement
- A risk register based on `../templates/risk-register.csv`
- A short explanation of how risks were prioritized
- Recommended controls and residual-risk notes
- References to any framework or guidance used

## Important
Label likelihood and impact ratings as estimates for the scenario. Do not present them as measured facts. If using NIST guidance, identify the specific publication and version.
